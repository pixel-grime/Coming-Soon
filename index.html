Teaching

Pilates

I'm a comprehensively certified Polestar Pilates instructor with a BA in Dance from Oberlin College. My sessions are personalized, grounded in movement science, and informed by my background in dance.

I'll meet you where your body is and build from there, creating a positive movement experience that develops strength, mobility, body awareness, and confidence.

Dance & Film

Dance Film

I make films where the camera dances. I design and weld camera stabilizers so that the lens can move with the body. I trained in Documentary Filmmaking at Prague Film School, and my work has been recognized on the international festival circuit.
