Movement Research & Collaboration

My work sits where bodies, cameras, and tools meet. I build equipment, and I study how people move and how technology can support that. I'm always open to talking with researchers, inventors, artists, and institutions working on similar questions.

Welded a Slider to Dance with Camera · Built in 2020, during lockdown. A camera slider I can control with my movement

Green Screen Bust · Built in 2024. A plaster cast of my face, neck, and shoulders, painted green and used as a projection surface for original dance films, B-roll, and digital renderings created by Sage Jensen.
